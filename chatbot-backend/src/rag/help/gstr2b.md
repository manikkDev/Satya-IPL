# GSTR-2B and ITC

GSTR-2B is the monthly auto-drafted statement of your purchases: invoices
suppliers reported in their GSTR-1 (and any actions you took in IMS) show
up here. Input tax credit (ITC) can only be claimed on invoices present in
GSTR-2B. Satya reconciles your booked invoices against imported 2B data
and flags purchases missing from 2B — ITC you may lose.
