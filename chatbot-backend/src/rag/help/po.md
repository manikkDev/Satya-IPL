# Purchase Order (PO)

A purchase order is the buyer's formal offer to a supplier: item lines,
quantities, rates, delivery schedule, taxes, payment terms and delivery
address. In Satya a PO is the anchor every later document is matched
against — deliveries (GRN), invoices and payments are checked line by line
against it. A PO can be amended (revisions are kept), short-closed
(remaining quantity cancelled) or partially fulfilled.

In the app: Purchase Orders → open a PO to see its lifecycle bars,
documents, money trail and issues.
