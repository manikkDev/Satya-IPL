# TDS on purchase of goods

Buyers deduct TDS of 0.1% on goods purchases above ₹50 lakh from a seller
in a year (when the buyer's turnover exceeded ₹10 crore the previous
year). Under the Income-tax Act 2025 the provision sits in section 393
from 1 Apr 2026 (it was 194Q under the 1961 Act). Deducted TDS makes a
payment look short — Satya treats it as a tax entry, not a missing amount.
