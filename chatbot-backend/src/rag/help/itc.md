# Input Tax Credit (ITC)

ITC is the GST you get back on purchases. Conditions: the invoice must
appear in GSTR-2B, goods must be received, the supplier must have paid the
tax, and you must file your return. If you don't pay the supplier within
180 days of the invoice date, the credit must be reversed with interest
(Rule 37) — Satya tracks that deadline for you.
